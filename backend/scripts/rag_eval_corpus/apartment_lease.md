# Apartment lease — Unit 4B, 12 Ly Tu Trong

## Term and rent

The lease runs for twelve months starting 1 March 2026. Monthly rent is 14,500,000 VND,
payable by bank transfer before the 5th of each month. Late payments incur a fee of
200,000 VND per day after a three-day grace period.

## Deposit

The tenant pays a security deposit equal to two months of rent. The deposit is refunded
within 30 days of move-out, minus the cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear.

## Pets

Tenants may keep one cat or one small dog under 10 kg. A separate refundable pet deposit
of 3,000,000 VND is required. Exotic animals and reptiles are not permitted.

## Maintenance and repairs

The landlord handles structural repairs, plumbing and the air conditioner service twice a
year. The tenant replaces light bulbs and keeps drains clear. Emergency maintenance hotline:
0903 555 218, available 24 hours.

## Termination

Either party may end the lease early with 60 days written notice. If the tenant leaves
early without notice, one month of rent is forfeited from the deposit.

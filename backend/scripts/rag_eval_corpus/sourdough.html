Sourdough baking notes

These are my notes from a year of baking country loaves at home with a starter named Bubbles.

Feeding the starter

Feed the starter twice a day at a 1:1:1 ratio of starter, flour and water by weight. Use a mix of 80 percent bread flour and 20 percent whole wheat. It peaks about five hours after feeding at 27 degrees Celsius.

Bulk fermentation

Bulk fermentation takes four to five hours in a warm kitchen. Do four sets of stretch and folds, thirty minutes apart, during the first two hours. The dough is ready when it has grown by about half and shows bubbles on the sides.

Baking

Preheat the Dutch oven at 250 degrees Celsius for 45 minutes. Bake 20 minutes with the lid on, then 25 minutes with the lid off until the crust is deep brown. Let the loaf cool for an hour before slicing.
